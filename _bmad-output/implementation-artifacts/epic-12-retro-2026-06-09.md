## What was rougher — the sharp lessons (all caught UPSTREAM of smoke)

Every defect this epic was caught by QA or CR; smoke caught zero escapes. The catches are the lessons:

1. **`CR-12.3-H1` (the marquee) — a wrong tool-SEMANTIC claim in an agent-consumed asset.** The cross-project play documented `post_announcement` as GRANTING membership ("acting = joining"). That is false against the shipped contract: `post_announcement` GATES on membership (`requireMembership` → `NOT_A_MEMBER`); only `reply` grants-on-act. Because the play targets *another* project's sub-board (the agent is not a member), an agent following it verbatim would hit `NOT_A_MEMBER`. **The error originated in the Story 12.3 Dev Notes** (the lead's own source-fact) and rode through dev. The Rule-10 content-guards pin tool NAMES to the live surface — they are structurally blind to a wrong SEMANTIC claim about how a named tool behaves. CR caught it against core ground truth (`post-announcement.ts:11`, `reply.ts:11-14`) and routed the proto-room branch through `join_board` first; the 12.5 post-skill then inherited the corrected join-first pattern. → **Rule 22.**
2. **12.2 — coherence HIGHs from a step insertion + renumber.** Adding the announce-or-join "Step 5" and renumbering left the most-travelled (recorded-handle) LOGIN path still saying "Bootstrap is done" — skipping the new step — plus a dangling "go to step 4" reference. The content-guard pins step/token PRESENCE, not cross-reference coherence or path-traversal, so it stayed green on an incoherent asset. CR caught both, fixed in the canonical + the byte-identical inlined copy. → **Rule 23.**
3. **Call-form blind spots recurred (Rule 18 reinforcement).** QA caught the dev's NFR5 read-only scan (`` `tool{ ``) missing paren-form and bare-brace write calls (12.4), and the snippet phantom regex needed broadening to the call-form-aware form (12.3). Same class as the Epic-8 call-form lesson — a guard must match every invocation form the asset uses. No new rule; Rule 18 already covers it and earned its keep twice this epic.
4. **Rule-20 false-green recurred every story** — lint/typecheck/test green while `prettier --check` was RED — caught at each gate because the FULL gate (incl. format) was re-run. Reinforces Rule 20; no new rule.

## Rules codified (Rule-1 step) → `.claude/rules/project-rules.md`

Both ratified by the Project Lead in this retro:

- **Rule 22** — An agent-consumed asset that asserts a tool's BEHAVIORAL SEMANTICS (gates-vs-grants, requires-vs-creates, side effects, ordering) must verify that claim against the tool's ACTUAL core behavior; a Rule-10 name-pinning content-guard is structurally blind to a wrong semantic claim. (CR-12.3-H1)
- **Rule 23** — Inserting or renumbering a step in an agent-executed asset requires a cross-reference + path-traversal COHERENCE check (every branch still reaches the new step; no dangling step references); a presence-pin content-guard does not catch flow incoherence. (Epic 12 Story 12.2)

Lessons 3 (call-form scans) and 4 (Rule-20 false-green) were judged already-covered by Rules 18 and 20 respectively — reinforced in this retro, not re-codified.
