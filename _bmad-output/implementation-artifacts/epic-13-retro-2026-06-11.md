## What went well

- **Fully automated, end-to-end.** The Project Lead's assessment (Q1): "Everything was automated, it went well." The `/epic-cycle` pipeline drove all 6 stories — lead-side create-story + ADR gate + per-story smoke, spawned dev/QA/CR agents — with zero clarification halts and zero smoke escapes.
- **The P1 flake fixes were root-caused, not papered over.** 13.2 traced the Shiki fault to `@shikijs/primitive@4.1.0`'s dual-grammar desync under concurrent cross-worker tokenization, Research-First'd for a patch (none), and *rejected* a blind version bump (would risk the AC-frozen inert class-span output) in favor of removing the concurrency (a serialized `markdown-serial` Vitest project). 13.1 unified the two Windows temp-dir teardown flakes into one shared retry/swallow helper. Both proven over N≥3 consecutive full-suite runs. → **Rule 24.**
- **Rule-8 reconciliations were surfaced, not steamrolled.** 13.4 recognized the AC's "migrate raw INSERT to append" over-stated the shipped invariant (append-only = no UPDATE/DELETE; the controlled-`created_at` proof tests are load-bearing and cannot move to `append`, which stamps `created_at` itself) and reconciled it explicitly in the Dev Agent Record + to the reviewer.
- **QA earned its keep on the seams.** QA stages repeatedly caught what the dev's guard missed: 13.2's collection-mapping drift guard (every highlighter-using file runs exactly once), 13.4's template-literal call-form coverage (Rule 18), 13.5's cross-surface equivalence matrix, 13.6's durable precise-token discrimination tests.
- **The 13.0 triage audit found 2 genuinely-open items the SCP's own count missed** (`5.1-roomid-cap-edge`, `10.3-join-project-inert`) — the retro-review gate doing exactly its job (triage every OPEN item, not just the ones a planning doc enumerated).
- **Rule 13 held the entire epic.** Even though this epic touched board-engine internals, the agent-facing surface (17 tools, closed 10-code error set, 10-event vocab) stayed byte-identical — every story verified `git diff HEAD -- packages/core/src/errors.ts packages/mcp-server/src` empty and the drift guards green.

## What was rougher — the sharp edges (all caught UPSTREAM of smoke)

Every defect this epic was caught by QA, CR, or the gate; smoke caught zero escapes. The Project Lead flagged nothing additional (Q2: "Nothing I can think of"). The records show:

1. **Rule-20 false-green recurred almost every story** — `prettier --check` RED while lint/typecheck/test were green (13.2, 13.4, 13.5, 13.6). Caught each time *because* the FULL gate was re-run (Rule 20 working as intended), but it remained a persistent papercut. No new rule — Rule 20 already covers it and earned its keep four times this epic.
2. **A guard's own config comment became a live ESLint directive** (13.4) — a literal `// eslint-disable-next-line carve-out.` phrase in `eslint.config.js`'s own comment was parsed as a real directive (RED: rule `carve-out.` not found). Reworded so the phrase isn't the comment's first token. Niche one-off; stays in the story record, not codified.
3. **`-next-line` eslint-disable carve-outs are Prettier-unstable** (13.4) — reflow can move the directive off its target node; switched to a position-stable block disable/enable pair. A narrow tactical lesson (documented here); judged too specific for a numbered rule.

## Rules codified (Rule-1 step) → `.claude/rules/project-rules.md`

Ratified by the Project Lead in this retro:

- **Rule 24** — A flake fix must ELIMINATE the race (root cause), proven by **N≥3 consecutive full-suite runs + a Rule-7 mutation that the assertion still fires** — never mask it; a single green pass proves nothing for an intermittent flake. A best-effort swallow is legitimate only as provable post-assertion hygiene; when the root cause is a library fault, Research-First the installed version and prefer removing the concurrency over a blind version bump. (Epic 13 Stories 13.1 + 13.2.)

Lessons 1 (Rule-20 false-green) and the two niche tactical items (config-comment-as-directive, `-next-line` Prettier instability) were judged already-covered (Rule 20) or too narrow to codify — reinforced/recorded, not re-codified.
